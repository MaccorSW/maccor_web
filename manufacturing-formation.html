OVERVIEW

Production-Scale Formation Without Compromise

Formation — the first charge/discharge of a new battery cell — is one of the most critical and energy-intensive steps in lithium-ion manufacturing. It defines the SEI layer, calibrates cell capacity, and flags defects before they reach customers. Getting it wrong is expensive. Getting it right at scale requires Maccor.

The Series 5302 is engineered for continuous, high-volume operation. With up to 2,048 independent channels per system, and 100% duty cycle capability, and that recycles power back to the grid, it delivers the throughput and economics required for competitive battery manufacturing.
