FAQ

Battery Testing Questions, Answered

Quick answers to common questions from battery R&D, EV, and manufacturing teams.

What is a battery cycler?

A battery cycler is a battery test system that charges and discharges cells, modules, or packs under controlled conditions to measure capacity, efficiency, and cycle life.

What battery formats can Maccor test systems support?

Maccor systems support testing for battery cells, modules, and packs, with scalable channel counts and configurations for many form factors and chemistries.

Do you offer manufacturing formation and grading systems?

Yes. Maccor provides automated formation solutions designed to scale from pilot lines to high-volume lithium-ion battery manufacturing.

Can Maccor integrate cyclers with thermal chambers and safety controls?

Yes. FUSION integrated systems combine cycler hardware, chamber integration, and safety controls into a pre-validated turnkey solution.

Since 1987, Maccor has been the trusted partner for battery testing excellence. Based in Tulsa, Oklahoma, we design and manufacture automated test systems that drive innovation worldwide.
