OVERVIEW

Accuracy You Can Stake Your Quality Program On

Quality control testing is the last line of defense before batteries reach customers. Whether you're validating incoming cells from a supplier or running outgoing inspection on finished packs, the accuracy of your test system directly impacts the reliability of your product guarantee.

The Maccor Series 4000 and 4000H are the industry-standard platforms for production QC. With 0.02% voltage accuracy across the full operating range, 1 to 192 independent test channels per system, and flexible fixture support for any cell format, these systems handle the volume and accuracy that serious QC programs require.
