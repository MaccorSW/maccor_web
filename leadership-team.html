Founder & President
AM
Founder, Owner & President

Andy MacKay

Maccor Inc. · Tulsa, Oklahoma · Founded 1987

Andy MacKay founded Maccor with a singular goal: build the best battery testing systems available and support them comprehensively. Under his leadership, Maccor became a recognized pioneer in automated battery and cell test equipment, serving customers worldwide across research, manufacturing, quality control, and energy storage applications.

Andy continues to guide the company's long-range vision, helping ensure Maccor remains focused on technical excellence, customer support, and durable growth.

Senior Leadership

Senior Vice Presidents

Promoted in March 2025, three long-serving Maccor veterans now lead the company's operations, manufacturing, and global commercial functions.

BK
Brian King
Senior Vice President

Brian leads all operations at Maccor's corporate headquarters in Tulsa, Oklahoma. A 34-year veteran of the company, he progressed through Maccor's manufacturing organisation to become Director of Manufacturing & Quality Assurance before his 2025 promotion. His technical expertise and deep institutional knowledge have been instrumental in optimising production efficiency and maintaining the precision standards that Maccor's equipment is known for worldwide.

Before Maccor, Brian served as a Nuclear Weapons Specialist in the U.S. Air Force — a background that shaped his disciplined approach to process integrity and operational accountability. He holds a B.S. in Electrical Engineering Technology from Oklahoma State University.

Jon Causey
Senior Vice President

Jon oversees operations at Maccor's satellite locations, driving the company's vision and global expansion. JK
Justin Kearns
Senior Vice President

Justin oversees Maccor's global Sales and Customer Support operations from Tulsa, Oklahoma. His 17 years at Maccor — progressing from Sales & Applications Engineer to Manager of Global Customer Support — are complemented by 13 years at Energizer Batteries, where he led teams in battery manufacturing and testing operations. That combination gives Justin a rare perspective: deep expertise in both what customers are trying to achieve with their batteries and how to test them effectively.

In his SVP role Justin is responsible for Maccor's commercial relationships worldwide, the technical applications team, and the customer support infrastructure that keeps thousands of Maccor systems running in over 60 countries. He holds a B.S. from Northwest Missouri State University.

What Drives the Team

🇺🇸

Made in America

Every Maccor system is designed, engineered, and manufactured in Tulsa, Oklahoma. That is not a marketing position — it is how Andy MacKay founded the company and how it has always operated.

🔬

Engineering First

The leadership team's backgrounds span physics, electrical engineering, battery manufacturing, and quality assurance. Technical depth is the basis of every product and support decision Maccor makes.

🤝

Long-Term Relationships

The three SVPs have a combined 68 years at Maccor. Maccor's representatives average over ten years with the company. Long tenure is a deliberate principle, not a coincidence.

🌍

Global Reach, Local Support

Systems operating in over 60 countries, supported directly from Tulsa in the Americas and through experienced regional partners worldwide — each with deep hands-on Maccor product knowledge.
