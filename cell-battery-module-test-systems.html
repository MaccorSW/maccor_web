FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Series 4000 and Series 4000H?

The Series 4000 supports up to 96 channels at 1,400A and 14,400W with 1 or 4 current ranges, making it the standard choice for cell and small-module testing. The Series 4000H is the high-power variant, supporting up to 96 channels at 2,400A and 28,800W with 2 or 3 current ranges — designed for high-current module and pack testing where you need to test at full operating power levels.

How many channels can a Maccor system support?

The Series 4000 and 4000H support 1 to 96 channels per system, configurable at time of order. Multiple systems can be networked together through MacTest32 for larger-scale test programs. The Series 6500 supports 1 to 8 channels per system.

What software do Maccor battery test systems use?

All Maccor systems — Series 4000, 4000H, 6500, desktop units, and FUSION systems — run MacTest32, Maccor's proprietary test control software. Test procedures, data files, and analysis tools are fully portable across every system in your fleet. There is no retraining required when you add a new system or upgrade your platform.

What is the minimum step time for Maccor test systems?

The Series 4000 and 4000H offer minimum step times of 2ms, 5ms, or 10ms depending on configuration. The Series 6500 has a minimum step time of 10ms. Fast step times are essential for accurate HPPC testing, dynamic profiles, and characterization work where the hardware must execute the protocol as written — not approximated.

Can Maccor systems be integrated with thermal chambers?

Yes. Maccor systems with the temperature monitoring option support direct thermocouple integration for logging and test-flow branching. For a fully integrated and pre-validated solution, the FUSION Integrated System combines a Maccor cycler, thermal chamber, and safety controls in a single turnkey package — deployed and tested as a unit before leaving the factory.

How widely deployed are Maccor systems?

Maccor battery test systems are deployed in over 50 countries across more than 1,000 facilities worldwide — from the world's largest EV and consumer electronics manufacturers to leading university research programs and national laboratories. The Series 4000 platform has been in continuous production since 1987, making it the longest-running and most widely trusted battery test platform in the industry.

Where are Maccor systems manufactured?

All Maccor battery test systems are designed, built, and calibrated at our facility in Tulsa, Oklahoma, USA. Every system is NIST-traceably calibrated and factory acceptance tested before shipment. Manufacturing in-house gives us direct control over quality and allows our engineering team to support customers directly — not through intermediaries.
